01.about

I'm a Technical Lead with 9 years of experience building and leading MEDplat at Argusoft. I spend my time on system design and I still code every day in Java, Spring Boot, Flutter, Dart and PostgreSQL. I also lead a 40-engineer group and own delivery end to end, from government client requirements to production.

I designed the platform's configuration-driven architecture, which is what lets a new state go live in a week and a new form ship in a day. I also led the migration from Android to Flutter and built voice- and AI-assisted workflows that cut form entry from minutes to seconds for health workers in low-connectivity areas.

I care most about software that holds up offline, on low-end phones, and at population scale.
